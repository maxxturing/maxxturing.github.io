03 — Fractional COO

Operations for founders — without a full-time hire.

Most early-stage teams don't need a full-time COO. They need someone who has done it before to come in, build the operational spine of the company, and hand it back running. That's what I do.

01

Operations & process

The systems that stop things falling over: planning rhythms, tooling, documentation, reporting and the day-to-day processes a growing team actually sticks to.

02

Hiring & team building

Role scoping, sourcing, interview processes and onboarding — plus the contracts, payroll and people admin that come with your first handful of hires.

03

Finance & funding

Budgets, forecasts, runway modelling and bookkeeping that stands up to scrutiny — and support on grants, pitch material and investor reporting.

04

Product & delivery

Turning a roadmap into shipped work: prioritisation, scoping, sprint cadence and managing engineering or agency partners to a deadline.

05

Programmes & events

Designing and running accelerators, cohorts, demo days and conferences — from logistics and speakers through to measurement.

06

Launches & brand ops

Standing up a new venture or brand quickly: website, identity, comms, and the operational setup behind a credible public launch.
